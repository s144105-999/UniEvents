About UniEvents

UniEvents is a university events and registration platform created for students at Sultan Qaboos University.

Our website helps students explore university events, view event details, and submit registration forms.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make university events easier to find and more accessible to students.

Our Objectives

  • Help students discover university events.
  • Provide clear information about each event.
  • Make event registration easier.
  • Allow students to share their feedback.
  • Help organizers manage event information.

Website Features

  1. Browse available university events.
  2. View event dates, times, and locations.
  3. Submit an event registration form.
  4. View sample registered events.
  5. Send feedback about university events.
